# Rooted Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 10 October 2026**

Rooted is a personal journaling app designed to help you record your thoughts, track your moods, save photos, and keep a dream journal.

## 1. Your personal information

Rooted is designed to keep your personal content private. Journal entries, mood records, dream entries and photos are stored on your device and, where enabled and supported by your settings, in your personal Apple iCloud account.

The developer does not operate a server to collect or store your journal content.

## 2. iCloud and data storage

If iCloud syncing or backup is enabled, your information may be stored or synced through Apple's iCloud services, according to your Apple account settings and Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

The availability and handling of iCloud data depend on how your device and the app are configured.

## 3. Data collection and sharing

Rooted does not include developer-added analytics or crash-reporting tools. It is not designed to send your journal entries, moods, dreams or photos to the app developer. The developer does not sell your personal journal content.

Apple may process information as necessary to provide iCloud and other Apple services you choose to use.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

Your content remains on your device and may also remain in iCloud or backups, depending on your settings. You can delete content within the app where supported and manage your device's iCloud data and backups through Apple settings.

Deleting content from the app may not automatically remove every copy held in backups or other synced locations.

## 5. Children's privacy

Rooted does not operate its own server for collecting journal content and is not designed to collect personal information through a developer-operated service.

## 6. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Rooted develops. Any revised version will be published at the same policy address with an updated effective date.
